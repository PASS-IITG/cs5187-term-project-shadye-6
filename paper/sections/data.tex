\paragraph*{Data Availability.}
\label{sec:data}
The complete replication package for \tool is provided in the supplemental repository at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/qtfuzz-data-anon/}.
The package includes target harnesses, bandit mutation schedulers, in-memory shims, standalone defect reproducers, seed corpora, and evaluation scripts.

\paragraph*{Use of Generative AI.}
A generative AI assistant helped format LaTeX tables and check grammar in early drafts.
The authors verified all technical statements, equations, citations, and measurements produced by the software and test scripts.
